Byoungkwan Lee

Professor of Advertising & Public Relations @Hanyang University

I am a professor in the Department of Advertising and Public Relations at Hanyang University. I served as the founding president of the Korea Health Communication Association, and for many years sat on the policy communication advisory committee of the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency. My field is health communication, with a sustained focus on evaluating the effects of public health communication campaigns and on statistical methodology — data science, simulation and machine learning among them. Most recently my attention has turned to metascience: research on the process of science itself.
